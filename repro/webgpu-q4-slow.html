WebGPU 4-bit timing test

Runs EmbeddingGemma 2 (onnx-community/embeddinggemma-2-ONNX, text part only) through Transformers.js 4.3.2 and onnxruntime-web, and times one forward pass per word. On a Pixel 10 Pro (Imagination graphics chip) the 4-bit model on WebGPU takes about 20 s a word, while the 16-bit model takes 0.24 s on WebGPU and 0.13 s on WASM.

Each button downloads that model once (4-bit about 175 MB, 16-bit about 543 MB) and then runs three words. Run one at a time.
